# CTF Writeups

This is where I keep track of the CTFs and vulnerable machines I work through.

I started this repository to document my journey into offensive cybersecurity, track my progress, and build a personal knowledge base along the way. 
By writing down each challenge, technique, and lesson learned, I aim to develop a deeper understanding of what I'm doing rather than simply focusing on getting things to work. 
I also want to create a record of my growth, something I can look back on in six months or a year to see how my skills, methodology, and approach to solving security challenges have evolved.

## A note on ethics
Everything here was done against machines built for this purpose — TryHackMe, HackTheBox or systems I had explicit permission to test. 
Flags and credentials found along the way are intentionally left out or redacted — the point of these writeups is the methodology, not the answer key.
